AJPW: SUPER GENERATION CALLS OUT MIRACLE VIOLENCE
Sapporo, Nakajima Sports Center | Segment 9 (4:00)

Stan Hansen is still on his back outside the ring, and Terry Gordy is
dragging him toward the aisle. Mitsuharu Misawa stays in the ring with
Jumbo Tsuruta. The two men who were supposed to meet for the Triple
Crown at Budokan stand a few feet apart and say nothing to each other.

The ring announcer hands Misawa the microphone. He waits for the
building to quiet down.

MISAWA: Sapporo. Thank you.

The crowd cheers. Misawa looks toward the entrance.

MISAWA: Kobashi. Come out here.

Kenta Kobashi walks out in his warm-up jacket and climbs in. Misawa
puts a hand on his shoulder.

MISAWA: On Tuesday in Yokohama, Kobashi and I fight for the World Tag
Team titles. Williams. Gordy. Those belts were ours. We are coming to
take them back.

Kobashi takes the microphone.

KOBASHI: I am not Kawada. But I will not lose.

The crowd chants for Kobashi. Steve Williams walks out onto the ramp
with a World Tag belt over each shoulder. Gordy, still helping Hansen
up, stops beside him. Williams holds both belts over his head.

STEVE WILLIAMS: You want these? Come to Yokohama and try. And Jumbo,
you look at me. Budokan. Six days.

Williams points at Jumbo. Jumbo doesn't move. Misawa and Kobashi step
to the ropes, and Jumbo steps between them and the ramp, facing
Williams, with his back to Misawa.

Williams laughs, drops one belt over Gordy's shoulder and walks Hansen
to the back. Jumbo stays at the ropes until they are gone, then leaves
the ring without a word to Misawa. Misawa and Kobashi raise their hands
to the crowd as the show goes off the air.
