MIRACLE VIOLENCE TAKES THE GOLD
Summer Action Series, Night 8 | Okayama, Okayama Budokan | 1991-07-15
Venue: Okayama Budokan
City: Okayama, Japan
Date: July 15, 1991
Attendance: 2,100
Average ticket: $20.00
Gate: $42,000.00
Merchandise: $4,200.00

Toshiaki Kawada's last night in All Japan ended with the World Tag Team
Championship around someone else's waist.

Mitsuharu Misawa and Kawada defended against Steve Williams and Terry Gordy
in the main event at the Okayama Budokan, and for twenty minutes the
champions gave everything they had. Kawada kicked Williams until his shins
were red. Misawa hit the elbows. It wasn't enough. At 20:21 Gordy caught
Kawada, lifted him up and drove him down with a powerbomb, and the referee
counted three. The Miracle Violence Connection are the new World Tag Team
Champions.

Williams and Gordy took the belts and left. Misawa stayed on his knees in
the ring. Kawada got up, looked at his partner for a long moment, and
walked to the back by himself. He didn't take the microphone. He didn't
come back out.

One match earlier, Jumbo Tsuruta put the Triple Crown on the line against
Kenta Kobashi. Kobashi took the Jumbo Knee and kicked out. He took it again
and kicked out again, and the Okayama crowd was on its feet. Jumbo finally
ended it with a backdrop driver at 18:55. Jumbo keeps the title, and Steve
Williams gets him at the Nippon Budokan on July 27.

The Fantastics earned a title shot of their own. Tommy Rogers rolled up Dan
Kroffat at 17:53 of a non-title match with the All Asia Tag Team Champions,
and Bobby Fulton and Rogers will challenge the Can-Am Express at Budokan.

Yoshinari Ogawa beat Joe Malenko with a small package at 15:26, even with
Dean Malenko getting involved from the floor. There was no handshake. Akira
Taue dropped Davey Boy Smith with the nodowa otoshi. Dynamite Kid beat Too
Cold Scorpio with the diving headbutt in a fight that had the building
roaring, and Tsuyoshi Kikuchi and Masao Inoue, the young boys who stole
Hiroshima, did it again by beating Richard Slinger and Billy Joe Travis.

Misawa has a new partner coming. The tour moves to Kumamoto on Wednesday.
