AJPW: MISAWA WANTS ONE MORE
Yokohama, Yokohama Cultural Gymnasium | Segment 8 (3:00)

Steve Williams and Terry Gordy walk up the aisle with the World Tag
belts. Kenta Kobashi is still on the mat. Mitsuharu Misawa helps him to
the corner, then takes the microphone from the ring announcer.

MISAWA: Williams. Gordy. You are the champions tonight.

The crowd quiets down.

MISAWA: Thursday in Osaka. No belts. Kobashi and I against you. If we
win, we fight for those titles again.

Gordy stops on the ramp and looks back. Williams pulls him on toward the
curtain without answering. Kobashi gets to his feet and raises a hand
to the crowd. Misawa stays in the corner beside him as the show goes off
the air.
