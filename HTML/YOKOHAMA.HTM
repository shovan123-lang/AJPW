MIRACLE VIOLENCE HOLD ON, AND MISAWA WANTS ONE MORE

Mitsuharu Misawa and Kenta Kobashi got their shot at the World Tag Team
titles in Yokohama, and for twenty-three minutes it looked like Super
Generation might pull it off. Kobashi took the worst of Steve Williams
and Terry Gordy and kept getting up. Misawa saved him more than once.
But at 23:25 Williams picked Kobashi up for the Doctor Bomb and drove
him down for the three. The Miracle Violence Connection are still the
champions, and Williams still has not been pinned on this tour.

Misawa wasn't finished. He helped Kobashi to the corner, took the
microphone and told the champions he wants them again on Thursday in
Osaka. No belts. If Super Generation win, they get another title match.
Gordy stopped on the ramp and looked back. Williams pulled him through
the curtain without a word.

HANSEN FLATTENS TAUE, THEN JUMBO

Two nights after Misawa pinned him in Sapporo, Stan Hansen took it out
on Jumbo Tsuruta's side of the ring. Hansen and Dory Funk Jr. beat Jumbo
and Akira Taue when Hansen caught Taue with the Western Lariat at 15:30.
Then he turned around and laid Jumbo out with another one after the
bell. Jumbo and Hansen meet one on one in Osaka on Thursday.

DYNAMITE'S HEADBUTT DOES IT AGAIN

The British Bulldogs and Johnny Ace beat the Fantastics and Too Cold
Scorpio in the best match of the night. It went nearly eighteen minutes
before Dynamite Kid came off the top rope with the flying headbutt on
Bobby Fulton at 17:52. It was one of Davey Boy Smith's last nights in
All Japan. The Fantastics still get their All Asia title shot at
Budokan on Saturday.

KROFFAT STEPS IN

Masanobu Fuchi was not in the building, and Dan Kroffat stepped in as
Rusher Kimura's partner against Abdullah the Butcher and Giant Kimala
II. Kroffat dropped Kimala II with a backdrop suplex and pinned him at
13:39. Abdullah came back in with the fork after the bell and busted
Kroffat open before the young boys could pull him off. Kroffat and Doug
Furnas defend the All Asia Tag titles at Budokan in four days.

INOUE TAKES THE RUBBER MATCH

Masao Inoue and Billy Joe Travis have traded wins all tour. In Yokohama
Inoue settled it, rolling Travis up at 9:37 in an opener that had the
crowd on its feet. Inoue goes to Budokan with the win.

OGAWA EVENS IT UP

Two nights after Joe Malenko choked him out, Yoshinari Ogawa got the
last word. He and Tsuyoshi Kikuchi beat the Malenkos when Ogawa pinned
Joe at 16:34.

AROUND THE RING

Richard Slinger beat Haruka Eigen with the Chattanooga Choo Choo at
11:23.

Next for All Japan: Osaka on Thursday, July 25. Jumbo Tsuruta faces Stan
Hansen, and Super Generation get Miracle Violence again, no titles on
the line. The tour ends at Budokan on Saturday.
