MISAWA PUTS HANSEN DOWN, THEN CALLS OUT THE CHAMPIONS

All Japan's northern swing opened in Sapporo with Mitsuharu Misawa and
Jumbo Tsuruta on the same side of the ring, and they left together as
winners. After 18 hard minutes with Stan Hansen and Terry Gordy, Misawa
survived a Hansen bearhug, broke free and dropped him with the Tiger
Driver for the pin at 18:38. Pinning Hansen is something very few men in
this company can say they've done.

Then Misawa took the microphone and called Kenta Kobashi to the ring.
On Tuesday in Yokohama, Super Generation challenges the Miracle Violence
Connection for the World Tag Team titles. Steve Williams answered from
the ramp with both belts over his head and a warning for Jumbo: Budokan
is six days away. Jumbo stood between Misawa and the ramp and said
nothing. When Williams and Gordy were gone, he left the ring without a
word to his partner.

RUDE IS BACK, AND HE'S A CHAMPION

Two nights after winning the WCW United States title in Charleston,
West Virginia, Rick Rude came back to Japan with the belt and the best
match of the night. Doug Furnas pushed him for twelve minutes, with Dan
Kroffat urging him on from the corner, but Rude caught him with the
Rude Awakening for the pin at 12:31. After a hard tour, Rude finally
looks like the star he came here as. Johnny Ace is next, at Budokan.

FUNK TAPS FUCHI

Dory Funk Jr. stood beside Steve Williams again, and again it worked.
Funk tied up Masanobu Fuchi in the spinning toe hold and Fuchi had to
give up at 16:25. Williams and Funk beat Fuchi and Akira Taue, and
Williams still hasn't been pinned on this tour.

ACE SHARPENS UP FOR RUDE

Johnny Ace opened the night with a strong win over Tsuyoshi Kikuchi,
pinning him with the Implant DDT at 10:24.

THE FANTASTICS GET ONE BACK

Tommy Rogers rolled up Davey Boy Smith at 13:08 to give the Fantastics a
win over the British Bulldogs. The Fantastics get their All Asia Tag
title shot at Budokan.

THE MALENKOS' REVENGE

Two nights after Yoshinari Ogawa pinned Dean Malenko, Joe Malenko evened
the score. With Dean in his corner, Joe locked Ogawa in the Final Lesson
and wouldn't let go. Ogawa passed out, and the referee stopped it at
14:38.

AROUND THE RING

Kenta Kobashi had an easy night before Yokohama. He and Masao Inoue beat
Too Cold Scorpio and Billy Joe Travis when Kobashi flattened Travis with
the running lariat at 10:29. Abdullah the Butcher and Giant Kimala II
were thrown out at 7:09 against Rusher Kimura and Haruka Eigen. Jumbo
Tsuruta, who has his own score to settle with Abdullah over Giant Baba,
came to ringside, and in the confusion Eigen went down over Jumbo's foot.
The referee had seen enough of Abdullah and Kimala II and disqualified
them.

Next for All Japan: Yokohama on Tuesday, July 23. The Miracle Violence
Connection defend the World Tag Team titles against Misawa and Kobashi.
