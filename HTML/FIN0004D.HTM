JUMBO SPEAKS: "I HEARD YOU, DOCTOR"

Hiroshima, Hiroshima Sun Plaza | Segment 6 (3:00)

Backstage, in front of an All Japan banner. Jumbo Tsuruta stands with the
Triple Crown belts over his shoulder, still in his warm-up jacket. He isn't
wrestling tonight. The interviewer holds the microphone up to him. Jumbo's
words are translated for the camera.

INTERVIEWER: Jumbo, you haven't said a word since Kanazawa. Steve Williams
beat Kawada, called your name, and you walked to the ring and just stared
at him. Then on Thursday in Nagoya your team beat his. Tonight, the fans
want to hear from you.

Jumbo smiles and takes his time before he answers.

JUMBO TSURUTA: In Kanazawa, Williams said many words. I don't need many
words. I went to the ring so he could look at me. Now he knows I am real.
Now he knows this is real.

JUMBO TSURUTA: I heard you, Doctor. I heard you in Kanazawa. In Nagoya, I
answered you. Taue, Kobashi and me. Your team lost, and your young partner
learned what my knee feels like. Tonight, Misawa's team faces you. Good.
Watch closely, because Misawa and I learned in the same ring.

He lifts the belts off his shoulder and holds them out toward the camera.

JUMBO TSURUTA: These three belts. Many men want them. Kawada wanted them in
Chiba. He is a good wrestler. He still lost. Williams wants them. He is
strong, maybe the strongest man on this tour. But wanting is not the same
as taking.

INTERVIEWER: So it's Jumbo against Williams at the Budokan on July 27?

JUMBO TSURUTA: If he wants Budokan, he will have Budokan. But first,
Monday, Okayama. Kenta Kobashi.

Jumbo looks away from the camera for a moment, as if he's picturing it.

JUMBO TSURUTA: Kobashi stood next to me in Nagoya. Monday, he stands across
from me. He is young, and he doesn't stop. I like that about him. On
Monday, I will find out how long he can keep going. Then I go to Tokyo, and
I find out about the Doctor.

He puts the belts back over his shoulder.

JUMBO TSURUTA: Williams, you say you want what is mine. In All Japan, we
don't talk about it. We find out in the ring.

Jumbo raises his fist and shouts "Ooh!" The fans watching on the arena
screen shout it back. He walks off without another word, and the camera
stays on the empty banner before cutting back to the ring for the main
event.
