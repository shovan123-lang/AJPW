Match observations: 

Fukuoka Sports Center
Fukuoka
July 19, 1991
Attendance: 4,500
Average ticket: $28.50
Gate: $128,250
Merchandise: $9,000

GORDY POWERBOMBS ROGERS, MIRACLE VIOLENCE KEEP THE GOLD

The Fantastics came to Fukuoka with nothing to lose and a shot at the
World Tag Team Champions. They left knowing exactly what Mitsuharu
Misawa and Kenta Kobashi are walking into in Yokohama. Tommy Rogers and
Bobby Fulton kept Steve Williams and Terry Gordy on the run for long
stretches, but at 18:28 Gordy got Rogers up for the powerbomb and that
was the end of it. Williams still has not been pinned on this tour.

JUMBO ANSWERS FUNK

Dory Funk Jr. stood with Williams and Gordy two nights ago in Kumamoto.
Tonight Jumbo Tsuruta made him pay for it, with the Triple Crown on the
line. Funk took the champion into his world of holds and kept him there,
but Jumbo would not stay down. At 16:40 he hooked Funk for the backdrop
driver and pinned him clean. Jumbo is still the Triple Crown Champion,
and Dr. Death is eight days away.

SUPER GENERATION ROLLS ON

Misawa and Kobashi had their first match as a team since Misawa named
Kobashi his partner, and it was a short night's work. Abdullah the
Butcher and Giant Kimala II tried to brawl them all over the building,
but Misawa caught Kimala II with the Tiger Driver and pinned him at
8:37. Misawa and Abdullah meet in a Texas Death Match at Budokan.

THE BULLDOGS ARE BACK

After a string of tough nights, the British Bulldogs got back on track
in one of the best matches of the night. Johnny Ace and Too Cold
Scorpio, who have been trying to beat each other all tour, had to work
together, and for thirteen minutes they did. Then Dynamite Kid came off
the top with the flying headbutt on Scorpio and that was that at 13:06.

OGAWA GETS HIS REVENGE

Two nights after the Malenkos stole a win with Dean's feet on the ropes,
Yoshinari Ogawa got even. With Joe Malenko in his brother's corner,
Ogawa caught Dean in a small package at 14:08 and held on for the three.
It was one of the two best matches of the night, and the crowd rose for
the young junior when the referee's hand came down.

CAN-AM ROLL ON

The All Asia Tag Team Champions beat Akira Taue and Masanobu Fuchi in a
non-title match when Dan Kroffat dropped Fuchi with the tiger driver at
13:24.

AROUND THE RING

Masao Inoue and Tsuyoshi Kikuchi opened the night by beating Billy Joe
Travis and Richard Slinger when Inoue rolled up Travis at 10:10. Inoue
and Travis have been trading wins all tour, and this one goes to Inoue.
