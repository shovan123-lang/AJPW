ALL JAPAN PRO WRESTLING: SUMMER ACTION SERIES REPORT
Hiroshima, Hiroshima Sun Plaza | July 13, 1991

SUPER GENERATION ANSWERS JUMBO

Two nights after Jumbo Tsuruta's side beat the Miracle Violence Connection
and Richard Slinger in Nagoya, Mitsuharu Misawa's side got its turn against
the same three men in Hiroshima. Misawa made sure his answer was louder.

Misawa, Toshiaki Kawada and Kenta Kobashi went twenty minutes with Steve
Williams, Terry Gordy and Slinger in the main event of the Six-Man Tag
Special. Jumbo pinned the youngest man in the opposing corner on Thursday.
Misawa went after the biggest. He caught Gordy, lifted him up and dropped
him with the Tiger Driver for the three count at 20:39.

Williams was never pinned, and he made sure everyone noticed. When the bell
rang he stood in the middle of the ring with Misawa and neither man moved.
Then Williams turned, found the Triple Crown poster hanging over the
entrance and pointed at it. The message was for Jumbo, and Misawa was
standing right there to hear it.

JUMBO: "WE FIND OUT IN THE RING"

Jumbo didn't wrestle tonight, but he finally spoke. With the three belts
over his shoulder, the champion answered Williams for the first time since
the Doctor called him out in Kanazawa. If Williams wants Budokan, Jumbo
said, he'll have Budokan. But first comes Monday in Okayama, and Kenta
Kobashi. He finished with his fist in the air, and the Sun Plaza shouted it
back at him.

THE GATEKEEPERS HOLD AGAIN

The match of the night came in the middle of the card. Dean and Joe Malenko
teamed with Dory Funk Jr. against Yoshinari Ogawa, Tsuyoshi Kikuchi and
Akira Taue, and for sixteen minutes nobody in the building sat down. Ogawa
came closest to an upset when he rolled Joe up in a small package and had
the referee's hand coming down for three before Joe kicked out. It didn't
last. Joe trapped Kikuchi in the Final Lesson and Kikuchi had to submit at
16:22. The Malenkos are still the wall, but Ogawa has now been within a
fraction of a second of beating one of them, and he gets Joe on his own in
Okayama on Monday.

THE FORK COMES OUT AGAIN

Rusher Kimura and Masanobu Fuchi won their match with Abdullah the Butcher
and Giant Kimala II, but nobody will remember the result. Joe Malenko came
down to ringside and went after Kimura, and Abdullah pulled out the fork,
the same fork that put Giant Baba on a stretcher in Chiba. The referee
threw it out at 8:07. Abdullah wouldn't stop. It took the young boys to
drag him off Kimura, and on his way out Abdullah held the fork up to the
hard camera. Misawa vs. Abdullah at Budokan just got a little more
personal.

AROUND THE RING

Masao Inoue opened the show with the best win of his young career, rolling
up Billy Joe Travis at 8:22 two nights after coming up short in the All
Asia Tag title match in Nagoya. Too Cold Scorpio evened his account with
Johnny Ace, who handed him his first loss in Kanazawa, by hitting the
Flying Scorpio Splash at 10:25. And the British Bulldogs beat the
Fantastics when Dynamite Kid came off the top with the diving headbutt on
Bobby Fulton at 12:11.

The tour moves on to Okayama on Monday: Jumbo Tsuruta vs. Kenta Kobashi,
and Yoshinari Ogawa vs. Joe Malenko.
