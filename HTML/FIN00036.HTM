AJPW SUMMER ACTION SERIES - NIGHT 9
Kumamoto Prefectural Gymnasium - July 17, 1991

SEGMENT: Misawa Names His New Partner
(Follows Kenta Kobashi vs. Rick Rude)

Kobashi is still on one knee in the corner, chest heaving, after the
moonsault put Rude away. Rude is helped to the back by the young boys.
The ring announcer starts to read the decision again when the crowd
turns toward the entrance ramp.

Mitsuharu Misawa walks out in his green ring jacket. He isn't dressed
to wrestle yet; Hansen is still to come. No music. He walks slowly,
eyes on the ring the whole way. He climbs in, takes the microphone
from the announcer and waits for the crowd to settle.

MISAWA: Kumamoto.

(Pop.)

MISAWA: Two nights ago in Okayama, I lost the World Tag Team belts.
        Gordy and Williams took them. That is the truth, and I won't
        make excuses for it.

MISAWA: Kawada is gone. He made his choice. I won't talk about him
        tonight. He is not here.

(Some murmuring in the crowd. Misawa lets it pass.)

MISAWA: But Super Generation is not one man. It was never two men.
        It is anyone who is not afraid to stand in front of Jumbo,
        in front of Hansen, in front of the foreigners, and not
        step back.

(He turns to Kobashi, who has gotten to his feet in the corner.)

MISAWA: In Hiroshima, he stood next to me. In Okayama, he stood in
        front of Jumbo Tsuruta and took the knee and got up. Tonight
        he beat Rick Rude.

MISAWA: Kobashi.

(Kobashi steps forward. The crowd is starting to understand.)

MISAWA: Starting today, you are my partner.

(Big pop. Misawa holds out his hand. Kobashi looks at it for a long
moment, then grabs it with both hands and bows deeply. Misawa pulls
him up so they are eye to eye.)

MISAWA: Miracle Violence Connection. You have my belts. We are
        coming to take them back. Name the place.

(Misawa hands the microphone to Kobashi.)

KOBASHI: (still out of breath) Misawa-san... I will not let you down.
         Gordy! Williams! I am not afraid of you. Wherever you want.
         Whenever you want. We will be there.

(Kobashi throws the microphone down and raises Misawa's arm. Misawa
lets him, then raises Kobashi's arm in turn. The two stand in the
center of the ring as the crowd chants "MI-SA-WA! KO-BA-SHI!")

(Cut to commentary.)

COMMENTARY: Kawada leaves All Japan and a new Super Generation is
            born, here in Kumamoto. Mitsuharu Misawa and Kenta
            Kobashi, and the challenge has been laid down to the
            Miracle Violence Connection. And Misawa still has to
            face Stan Hansen tonight!
